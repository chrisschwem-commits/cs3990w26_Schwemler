Fighting Games

One of my hobbies is playing fighting games such as Street Fighter and Guilty Gear.
I like the competitive aspects of the hobby and enjoy watching and participating in tournaments.
Another aspect of this hobby that I enjoy is arcade machines and hardware, which are still a big part of the community.
Currently I have two arcade cabinets, one Neo-Geo, and the other being a Street Fighter 2: The World Warrior cabinet.

Coffee

Another hobby of mine is coffee and everything involved with it.
I enjoy tasting specialty coffee from all over the world; some of my favorites are from Kenya and Colombia.
I enjoy brewing coffee through different methods and trying to make the best coffee possible.
Currently I use the Hario V60 everyday, although sometimes I prefer using the Aeropress depending on the coffee.

Language Learning

Language learning is a hobby I have gotten into more recently.
So far I have only been attempting to learn one language - Japanese.
Currently I am a beginner but I have been making steady progress in the few months I have been learning.
The methods that I have been using thus far are a combination of traditional methods like textbooks and flash cards,
as well as more modern methods using the internet such as Youtube and various browser addons built for language learning.
