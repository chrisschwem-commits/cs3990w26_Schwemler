Coffee Roaster

One job that I would be interested in is the sourcing of specialty green coffee,
and then the roasting of said coffee into a finished product. One such company is Rogue Wave Coffee located in Edmonton.
I am personally a big fan of their style of coffee roasting (generally light roast) and have been enjoying their
coffee for a few years now. The job would entail working with coffee producers across the world to procure high
quality green coffee. Then having the coffee shipped to the roastery,
and roasting the green coffee with different roasting machines (like drum roasters). Click here for the company webpage

Software Engineer

The most typical job that computer science graduates pursue is something in software development/engineering.
Which typically involves designing, developing, and maintaining software. For me that probably wouldn't be working at a company
like Google or Meta but instead something smaller and preferably local. Remote work is also an option in this field which
makes it an attractive option for those wanting to live somewhere with a lower cost of living (like Grande Prairie).
One company could be Carmanah Signs located in Calgary.

Linux System Administrator

Similar to software engineering, jobs in IT are common for those with computer science degrees.
While IT is a broad discipline that encompasses many different jobs, the one I am most interested in is being a Linux
system administrator. The job would entail the setup, configuration, and maintenance of Linux systems such as servers.
An example of a company that hires Linux system administrators is Global Relay in Vancouver, BC.
